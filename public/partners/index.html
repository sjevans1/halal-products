HOW TO TAKE PART

A simple process, with independent checks.

  1. Share a public source.Use an official business, mosque or organizer link. Do not upload identity documents or personal details.
  2. We review the proposal.A suggestion is not an endorsement, ownership claim or halal verification.
  3. Information is checked and approved.We consult the source and appropriate responsible party before any public content change.
  4. Keep it current.Visitors can flag changed details and our editors revisit old references.

INTEGRITY ABOVE PLACEMENT

Transparency is non-negotiable.

Paid promotions will be clearly labelled and never imply independent certification. A business cannot buy a verified halal badge, and certifications must be sourced to the actual issuing body.
