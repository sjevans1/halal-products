Our privacy notice

Launch-stage information notice — 15 October 2026. This notice describes the early-access registration workflow proposed for the Tayyib Halal website. Registration remains disabled until the secure service and the responsible business contact details are confirmed.

What we collect

If you register after the form is activated, we collect the email address you submit and the date and time of your consent. Product ideas saved using the heart icon remain in your browser's local storage and are not sent to our servers.

Why we use it

We will use your email only to share Tayyib Halal launch news and product availability updates. You can withdraw consent using the unsubscribe method supplied in the messages we send when mailing is activated.

Who processes it

The planned website runs on Cloudflare Pages with Cloudflare Turnstile for anti-spam protection and Cloudflare D1 for launch-list storage. Turnstile may process technical browser signals to verify a human visitor. We do not operate a payment or order system at this stage.

Retention and contact

Retention, an accountable contact address, and formal unsubscribe handling must be approved before launch-list activation. Until then the registration form will remain unavailable. Once enabled, this notice will be updated to include the responsible company and contact methods.
