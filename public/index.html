✦   A GOOD PLACE TO BEGIN

Curious about halal?

You don't need to know everything to get started. Explore the essentials at your own pace—simple, welcoming, and free of jargon.

حلال
01

What does halal mean?

Halal means “permissible” in Arabic. In food, it concerns ingredients, sourcing and preparation in accordance with Islamic requirements.

02

What makes food tayyib?

Tayyib is about goodness and wholesomeness—an invitation to consider quality, care and the choices behind our food.

03

Is halal only for Muslims?

No. Everyone is welcome to explore halal foods. For Muslim customers, clear sourcing and certification information are especially important.

How will you verify halal standards? +

We intend to publish product-specific sourcing, ingredient and halal verification information before selling. Packaging artwork is not proof of third-party certification. Any certifier and certificate details will be displayed only after they have been independently verified.

Will you deliver across Jamaica? +

We're exploring distribution options. Locations, delivery areas, fees and timelines are not confirmed yet.

Can someone new to halal enjoy the products? +

Absolutely. Tayyib Halal is built to be approachable to anyone who values good food, transparency and intentional living.
